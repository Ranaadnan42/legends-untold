Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy describes "Atlas Untold Uploader", an internal tool operated by the owner of the Atlas Untold YouTube channel (youtube.com/@AtlasUntold-s1c).

What the tool does

The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos produced by the channel owner to the channel owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

The tool requests only the youtube.upload permission for the channel owner's own Google account. It does not read, collect, store or share any data about YouTube viewers or other users.

Data storage and deletion

The only stored credential is the channel owner's OAuth refresh token, kept as an encrypted secret in GitHub and used solely to upload videos. Deleting that secret, or revoking access at Google account permissions, removes the tool's access. No personal data is sold or shared.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
