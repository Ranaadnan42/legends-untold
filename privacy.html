Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy describes "Legends Untold Uploader", an internal tool operated by the owner of the Legends Untold YouTube channel (youtube.com/@LegendsUntoldTales).

What the tool does

The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos produced by the channel owner to the channel owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

The tool requests only the youtube.upload permission for the channel owner's own Google account. It does not read, collect, store or share any data about YouTube viewers or other users.

Data storage

The only stored credential is the channel owner's OAuth refresh token, kept as an encrypted secret in a private GitHub repository and used solely to upload videos. No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Contact

For questions, contact the channel owner through the About section of the Legends Untold YouTube channel.
